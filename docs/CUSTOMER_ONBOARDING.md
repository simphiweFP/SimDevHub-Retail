# Customer onboarding (opt-in)

The JWT-protected POST `/api/customers/me/onboard` is registered only when
an HTTPS OIDC authority and audience are configured and
`Retail__EnableCustomerOnboarding=true`. It accepts **no request body**.
Issuer (`iss`), subject (`sub`), verified email (`email`) and explicit
`email_verified=true` are taken from validated JWT claims only.

- The stable identity is the unique persisted pair (issuer, subject).
- The same identity and same verified email can register repeatedly without
  creating a second user.
- An inactive existing user or an existing account with a conflicting email
  fails closed. Email changes need a separate, auditable verified-update flow.
- A different issuer/subject with the SAME verified email creates a distinct
  user, not an implicit login to the older account.
- PostgreSQL's unique index protects identity binding during concurrency.
- New users are regular customer accounts, with NO store or staff membership.
- No password is generated by this API, and no provider secrets are persisted.

**Deployment limitations:** configure a trustworthy OIDC authority with proper
issuer, audience, token lifetime and signing-key validation. Review the
provider's email verification guarantee. Add an audited customer-account
recovery/linking workflow, MFA policy and abuse protections before production.
This does not enable payments or public Payfast checkout.
